# Student Guide

## 1. Log in

Student accounts are created by your teacher or the school administrator
— students do not register themselves. Go to the link your administrator
gave you (usually `.../slogin.php`) and enter:

- Roll number (ID)
- Stream (Natural Science / Social Science)
- The password your teacher gave you

If you don't have credentials yet, ask your teacher to create your
account (Admin → Students → Add Student). Forgot your password? Use the
**Forgot password?** link on the login page — you'll need to verify your
name and section — or ask your teacher to reset it for you.

## 2. Wait for the exam to start

After logging in you'll land on a waiting screen. It checks
automatically every couple of seconds and will take you into the exam
the instant your administrator starts it — you don't need to refresh.

## 3. During the exam

- Before you see any questions, you'll be asked to enter fullscreen —
  this is the exam's monitored mode. Leaving fullscreen, switching tabs,
  or trying to copy/paste is recorded and may be reviewed by your
  teacher, so keep the exam tab active and in fullscreen throughout.
- Your name, roll number, stream, and section are shown at the top at
  all times.
- The countdown timer is set by the server the moment you enter the
  portal, so refreshing the page will **not** give you extra time.
- Your answers and flags are **saved automatically** to the server every
  15 seconds (and again whenever you leave the tab) — you can see a
  "💾 Saved" indicator at the top. If your browser crashes or you lose
  connection, your progress is safe.
- Use **Flag question** to mark a question to revisit later, and
  **Clear my choice** to remove your current answer.
- The **Exam Overview** panel on the right shows every question; answered
  ones are shaded, flagged ones show a red corner marker. Click any box
  to jump straight to that question.

## 4. Submitting

Click **Finish Attempt...** to go to the review screen, where you can
see which questions are answered/unanswered before confirming. Once you
click **Yes, Submit**, your responses are locked permanently and you
cannot re-enter the exam.

If the timer reaches zero, your exam is **submitted automatically** with
whatever answers were last saved — there's no need to rush a manual
submission right at the buzzer.

## 5. After submitting

You'll see a screen confirming you've already taken that exam — you
cannot re-enter it. Your administrator can see your score once they
download the results.
